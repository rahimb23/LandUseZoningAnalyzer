Compatibility ratings are a screening aid based on Official Plan land use policy themes (transition in scale, protection of employment areas, sensitive land uses near industry). They are not a policy determination. Areas exclude road allowances and water, which carry no designation.

Zoning: City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 (Open Data, Open Government Licence – Toronto). Lands still under former municipal by-laws are not in this layer.

Official Plan: Toronto Land Use Spatial Data, parcel-level (2019–2021), University of Toronto Map & Data Library, CC BY-NC 4.0. Not an official City dataset: it reconstructs the 2019 land use maps and is not kept up to date. It has no Utility Corridors class.

▲ Unofficial and possibly out of date U of T reconstruction of the 2019 maps, not kept up to date. Check the City's current Maps 13 to 23. This Official Plan layer is a University of Toronto Map & Data Library reconstruction of the City's 2019 land use maps (parcels as of November 2021). It does not include Official Plan amendments made since 2019 and may misclassify some parcels. Confirm designations against the City's current Official Plan Maps 13 to 23.
